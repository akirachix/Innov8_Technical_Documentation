# Data Pipeline

```text
Camera
 ↓
Image quality gate
 ↓
Animal detection
 ↓
Species classification
 ↓
Lion identification
 ↓
Health assessment
 ↓
Risk analysis
 ↓
Alert / recommendation
```

Lion identification may use visible features such as whisker-spot patterns, scars, ear notches, mane structure, and body shape.

Health assessment may consider visible indicators such as rib exposure, flank depression, spinal/hip prominence, posture, gait, wounds, swelling, and abnormal weight distribution.

These outputs should be treated as decision support, not autonomous irreversible wildlife-management decisions.
