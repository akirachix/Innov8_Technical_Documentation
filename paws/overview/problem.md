# The Problem

During drought, water becomes concentrated in fewer locations. Wildlife and livestock are therefore increasingly likely to use the same remaining water sources.

The resulting chain is:

```text
Drought
  ↓
Water sources disappear
  ↓
Animals concentrate
  ↓
Livestock and lions converge
  ↓
Predation risk increases
  ↓
Livestock losses
  ↓
  
Retaliatory killing risk
```

PAWS is designed to move intervention earlier in this chain by turning field observations into operational intelligence.

> **Core idea:** detect the convergence before the conflict.
