# Users

## Conservation rangers

Rangers are field responders who need timely information about where risk is increasing and what intervention is required.

## HQ dispatchers

Dispatchers monitor the PAWS dashboard and coordinate field responses.

## Conservation managers

Managers use historical observations, risk information, and operational records to understand patterns and resource needs.

## Future / implementation-dependent users

Veterinary teams, water-truck operators, and partner organisations may be supported depending on the deployed implementation.
